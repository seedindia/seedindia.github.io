Announcement

The caravan travels to Bath

The third edition of SEED will be hosted by the Department of Economics at the University of Bath on 20–21 May 2026. The program and the list of presenters will be announced in the months ahead.

Following the format of previous editions, the symposium will run over two days and feature themed sessions on the economics of development — broadly defined — with 45–50 minute paper slots and ample time for discussion.
